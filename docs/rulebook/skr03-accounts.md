# SKR03 accounts: which account for which line

## How to choose

Book each invoice line to the account of DATEV's standard chart SKR03 (Standardkontenrahmen 03) that
fits what was bought, not who sold it: one supplier can deliver paper and a printer. The suggestion
comes from similar lines booked before; check it against this guide.

## Office and IT

- **4930 Bürobedarf:** office supplies: paper (Kopierpapier), toner, pens, folders.
- **4806 Wartungskosten für Hard- und Software:** maintenance and support contracts, IT service hours.
- **4964 Lizenzen und Konzessionen:** software licences and subscriptions (SaaS).
- **4920 Telefon** and **4925 Internetkosten:** phone and internet contracts.
- **4940 Zeitschriften, Bücher:** books, journals, subscriptions to publications.

## Assets and low-value items

- **4855 Sofortabschreibung geringwertiger Wirtschaftsgüter (GWG):** movable items up to 800 euros
  net each (§6 Abs. 2 EStG), for example a notebook or a monitor.
- **0410 Geschäftsausstattung** and **0420 Büroeinrichtung:** items above 800 euros net, which are
  capitalised and depreciated (Abschreibung, AfA).

## Premises, vehicles, services

- **4210 Miete**, **4240 Gas, Strom, Wasser**, **4250 Reinigung**, **4260 Instandhaltung betrieblicher
  Räume**.
- **4530 Laufende Kfz-Betriebskosten** (fuel), **4540 Kfz-Reparaturen**.
- **4950 Rechts- und Beratungskosten**, **4955 Buchführungskosten**, **4957 Abschluss- und
  Prüfungskosten**, **4945 Fortbildungskosten** (training).
- **4600 Werbekosten** (advertising), **4650 Bewirtungskosten** (business meals; only 70 % of the
  expense is deductible), **4360 Versicherungen**.

## Goods and freight

- **3400 Wareneingang 19 % Vorsteuer:** goods bought for resale or production.
- **3800 Bezugsnebenkosten:** freight and packaging on purchases; **4730 Ausgangsfrachten:** freight
  on our own shipments.

## When nothing fits

Use **4980 Sonstiger Betriebsbedarf** for small operating supplies and **4900 Sonstige betriebliche
Aufwendungen** only as a last resort, and tell the tax advisor (Steuerberater). The real chart of a
company comes from its tax advisor and may differ.
