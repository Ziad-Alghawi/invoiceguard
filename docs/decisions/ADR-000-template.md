# ADR-NNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or question are we facing? What forces are at play (requirements, constraints, risks,
costs)? Write it so a reader who has never seen the project can follow.

## Decision

What we decided, stated plainly in one or two sentences, followed by the details that matter.

## Alternatives

Other options we considered, one line each, with the main reason they were not chosen.

- **Option A:** why not.
- **Option B:** why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder because of this decision. Include the downsides we accept and anything
we will need to watch or revisit.
